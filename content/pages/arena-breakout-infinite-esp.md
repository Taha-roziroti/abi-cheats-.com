# Arena Breakout Infinite ESP

Corners, stairwells, and container rooms hide most of what matters in Arena Breakout Infinite. ESP is how you annotate that world without memorizing every spawn pattern.

This guide follows the seller’s Visuals tab: who is alive nearby, what they carry, which bots are padding the map, and which duffels might pay for your extract.

We keep examples tied to screenshots on this page so you can match each paragraph with a menu toggle—no copy-pasted feature dump from the homepage summary.

## Player ESP and In-Game Information

Player ESP is one of the central features of the Arena Breakout Infinite visual interface. It includes multiple display options that can show different types of information associated with players around the map.

The available options include player names, distance, health, weapon type, team index, view direction, and inventory information. Additional visual elements such as boxes, skeletons, head circles, and snaplines provide different ways to arrange the on-screen information.

These options make the ESP interface more flexible than a single fixed marker. You can explore the individual visual elements and focus on the information that best suits your preferred layout.

- Player names and distance
- Health bars and weapon type
- Team index and view direction
- Box and skeleton displays
- Head circles and snaplines
- Inventory lists and price information

## Arena Breakout Infinite AI ESP

Arena Breakout Infinite features both player-controlled opponents and AI activity. Having separate visual options for these categories makes the information displayed by the interface easier to organize.

AI ESP is included alongside Player ESP in the visual options. This gives the overlay another way to represent activity throughout a match while keeping the player-related settings available independently.

## Loot ESP, Inventory and Item Values

In Arena Breakout Infinite, the value of a raid is closely connected to what you find and collect. Containers, equipment, and valuable objects all contribute to the decisions made throughout a match.

Loot-related visual options provide inventory and price information that can be reviewed through the interface. The world visual settings also include player corpse and AI corpse ESP, with price, maximum-distance, and minimum-price controls.

This collection is particularly useful for players interested in the item-information side of the game. Rather than displaying only basic markers, the available options include details that help distinguish the information being shown.

## Understanding ESP and Wallhack Features

ESP and wallhack are commonly used to describe visual overlays that display information about the game environment beyond the ordinary on-screen view.

Within the Arena Breakout Infinite feature set, these terms refer to the visual tools covering player, AI, inventory, and loot information.

The interface combines several display types so that individual elements can be explored and configured independently. This makes the visual feature collection a central part of the overall ABI product.

## Visual Customization and Configuration

A useful interface should make the information you care about easy to distinguish. The available visual options include different indicators and display elements that can be arranged around your preferred presentation.

Additional controls include font size, crosshair settings, combat mode, and configuration management. Configurations can be created, saved, loaded, and deleted, making it easier to organize different arrangements of the available settings.
